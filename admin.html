Delete

This removes everything of this shop: its timed jobs (backups, checks), its link (phones can't open it any more), its script, its sheet with all items, bills and customers, its backups and vendor-bill files. The Directory and the dashboard stay, and you can create new shops as before. Your own shop can't be deleted here.
